Professional audio engineer and mixer with more than 30 years' experience in recording, editing, mixing, mastering and audio post-production.

My work has included music recording and mixing, broadcast and live audio, high-resolution live performance mixing and technical audio consultancy.

I bring a strong engineering approach to audio, with particular strengths in:

- Critical listening and audio quality assessment
- Recording, editing and mixing
- Pro Tools and Logic Pro
- Vocal and audio processing
- Audio quality control and technical troubleshooting
- Signal flow, acoustics and studio systems
- Working accurately under demanding deadlines

I am currently expanding my freelance work into spoken-word, voice and dialogue post-production, applying my professional audio engineering experience to voice, podcast, audiobook and other spoken-word projects.
